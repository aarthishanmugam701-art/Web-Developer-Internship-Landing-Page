01 — Overview

Build the web.
Not just homework.

The Program

Cognifyz Technologies invites passionate developers to join our 12-week Web Developer Internship — a structured journey from fundamentals to production-ready deployments.

Duration & Format

12 weeks of intensive learning, split into three phases: foundations (weeks 1–4), project sprint (weeks 5–9), and capstone launch (weeks 10–12). Flexible hybrid or fully remote options available.

What You'll Do

Collaborate on live client projects, participate in code reviews, attend weekly tech talks, and present your capstone to leadership. Graduate with a portfolio piece that actually ships.
